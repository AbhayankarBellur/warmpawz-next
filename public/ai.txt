Warmpawz is a technology platform connecting pet parents with verified pet care services across India.

Services include:
- Pet Adoption
- Veterinary Care & Consultations
- Pet Grooming
- Pet Training & Behaviour Consultation
- Pet Boarding & Daycare
- Pet Nutrition & Diet Planning
- Pet Resorts & Restaurants
- Pet Products & Commerce
- Sunset Services (end-of-life care)

Platform features:
- Verified and community-referred service providers
- Hyperlocal service discovery based on location
- Transparent onboarding for service providers
- Integrated payments and logistics
- Community feedback and shared learning
